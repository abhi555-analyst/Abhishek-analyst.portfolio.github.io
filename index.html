Complaint SLA Dashboard — Delhi/NCR Cluster

Problem

711 open service requests across a Delhi/NCR cluster with no single view of which ones were breaching SLA, in jeopardy, or clustered around a specific network fault.

Approach

Built a live Excel dashboard in three layers: a clean raw-data sheet, a Summary sheet of COUNTIF/COUNTA formulas (zero hardcoded numbers), and a Dashboard sheet with KPI cards and six linked charts — so every number recalculates the moment the source data changes.

Impact

Surfaced a clear hotspot the raw data was hiding: a single distribution point (MDF) accounted for 19% of all open complaints — 3x the next-highest — pointing straight at where a field team should be deployed first.

Closed SR Repeat-Complaint Analysis — 6-Month Trend

26,541 Closed SRs · Jan–Jun 2026Delhi/NCR FTTH
MonthsProblems
Problem

No clear view of whether closed complaints were staying resolved, which fault categories drove the most repeat visits, or how engineer-level repeat rates compared across the team.

Approach

Cleaned a 6-month closed-FR-SR export (26,541 records) and built COUNTIFS formulas cross-referencing SR status against month, problem sub-type, and engineer to isolate exactly where and when repeats were concentrated.

Impact

Found "Unplanned Outage" drives 62% of all volume and carries a 21.3% repeat rate — the single biggest lever for reducing repeats. Engineer-level repeat rates ranged from 14.5% to 22.2% among top performers, a real, comparable spread rather than noise.

Network Rehab Operations Performance Analysis

Merged survey · 319 + 279 RSUs · 5 zones
Featured · Live DashboardsNetwork Operations

Delhi/NCR — Config Survey & Rehab Completion Tracking

319 RSU · Config Survey · 27 Jan279 RSU · Rehab Completion · 6 Jan
Problem

Two parallel rehab workstreams across 5 zones — RSU configuration surveys and ODF / ODF Sagging / FAT+OTB / Bundling / Sagging remediation — with no single view of which zones were on track versus falling behind on either.

Approach

Built two linked Excel dashboards on top of the raw survey extracts, each with a clean zone-summary sheet, a Summary tab of SUM/AVERAGEIF formulas, and live charts — tracking survey completion, rehab completion, pending work by category, and Area Manager performance side by side.

Impact

Flagged the same zone (DEL-STH-2) as the weak point on both dashboards — lowest RSU survey completion (30.8%) and lowest rehab completion (80.4%) — turning two separate reports into one clear signal for where to focus field resources.
